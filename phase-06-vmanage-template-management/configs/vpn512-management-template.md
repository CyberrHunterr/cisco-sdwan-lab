# VPN 512 Management Template

The Device Template also includes VPN 512 and its management interface so that the management configuration remains represented after attachment.

## VPN

- VPN ID: `512`

## Management interface

- Interface: `eth0`
- Shutdown: `No`
- Tunnel Interface: `Off`
- Addressing: device-specific management addressing from the existing vEdge configuration

No transport color is configured on the management interface.
